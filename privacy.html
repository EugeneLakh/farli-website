Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers the website farli.net, operated by FARLI Software Studio ("FARLI", "we"). Each FARLI app has its own privacy policy, linked from its store listing.

In short

This website has no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no third-party fonts. Analytics runs only if you accept it in the banner. Until then, no analytics code is loaded and nothing is stored in your browser. We otherwise collect personal data only when you write to us.

What we receive

  • Technical logs. Like any website, our hosting provider may record technical data such as IP address, browser type and request time, to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not use it to identify visitors.
  • Your emails. If you write to help@farli.net, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

How we use it

We use your email and message only to reply and to handle your request. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or add you to any mailing list.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you accept, we use PostHog (EU cloud), a service provider acting on our behalf, to understand how the site is used.

  • What is collected: pages viewed, clicks on main buttons, the referring site, browser and device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and a random identifier stored in your browser (local storage and a cookie starting with "ph_"). We configure the service not to store full IP addresses.
  • What is not collected: no session recordings, no advertising or cross-site tracking, no selling or sharing of data.
  • Legal basis: your consent.
  • Retention: up to 12 months.

You can change your mind at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. Declining or withdrawing removes the identifier from your browser. We also treat the Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals as a refusal.

How long we keep other data

Emails are kept as long as needed to resolve your request and for a reasonable period afterwards. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to its use. Write to help@farli.net. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

Children

This website is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
