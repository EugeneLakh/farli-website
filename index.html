Principles

Clean, practical apps and games.

We build small things that do their job and get out of the way. Three rules shape every project, from a language trainer to a game for a retro console.

Simple

One job, done well. Each app solves a single everyday problem, with clear words on every button and nothing you have to learn first.

Fast

Instant launch, light footprint. We check startup time and app size on slow phones too, so nothing makes you wait.

Reliable

Tested on real devices. Every release goes through automated tests and a hands-on check before it reaches you.

About

Eugene Lakhmatov

Founder of FARLI Software Studio. Based in Lisbon, Portugal.

FARLI is a small independent studio. Most of our projects started at home: a trainer for the Portuguese language exam, a storybook for a child, a way to clean up a crowded photo library, games made as gifts for the kids in the family. We build what we need ourselves, then polish it until it is good enough to share.
